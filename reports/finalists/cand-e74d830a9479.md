## Problem

Cross-border FX corridors price congestion either through negotiated fees (opaque) or flat escalate-by-timeout rules (unresponsive to actual demand).

## Mechanism

FX settlement congestion becomes self-measuring: the demand index is derived entirely from on-chain queue state, and the escalation schedule couples price directly to that index, so the corridor prices its own scarcity without oracle trust. The refund reserve closes the loop by returning escalations to the population that paid them.

## Mathematical Model

- `X_t` (input, unit): anchor price level (~1000)
- `dX_t` (input, unit): anchor change that step
- `Q_t` (state, unit): queue demand index
- `Q_t1` (state, unit): next demand index
- `F_t` (state, unit): escalation premium level
- `F_t1` (state, unit): next premium level
- `W_t` (state, unit): congestion refund reserve
- `W_t1` (state, unit): next reserve
- `u_t` (auxiliary, unit): normalized queue pressure

- `u_t = sqrt(abs(dX_t)/max(X_t,1.0))` — scale-free queue pressure proxy
- `Q_t1 = clip(Q_t*(1-beta) + beta*(1000.0 + 300.0*u_t), 500.0, 2000.0)` — demand index EWMA around 1000 with pressure-driven lift
- `F_t1 = clip(600.0 + phi*(Q_t1-1000.0), 200.0, 1500.0)` — escalation premium is an increasing ladder in the index
- `W_t1 = clip(W_t + kappa_r*(F_t1-600.0) - min(0.5*(F_t1-600.0), 0.04*(W_t-1000.0)) - 0.03*(W_t-1000.0), 400.0, 2400.0)` — refunds capped per epoch and proportional to escalation contributed; stuffing's own escalations fund its refunds

**Parameters:** beta ∈ [0.05, 0.8] (default 0.3), phi ∈ [0.05, 0.8] (default 0.25), kappa_r ∈ [0.05, 0.9] (default 0.2)

**Open questions (§13):** should the ladder rungs be exponential rather than linear in Q?

**Critical assumptions:** queue depth is proxied by relative anchor moves; escalations recycle at rate kappa_r to congested settlers
