## Problem

Cross-border FX corridors settle through correspondent chains with locked liquidity and opaque spreads

## Mechanism

Corridor liquidity providers post escrowed inventory in both legs; a deterministic batch matcher clears corridor orders at a mid-market rate, an impact check bounds the per-batch price impact against posted depth, and a rebate pool pays providers from the matched spread

## Mathematical Model

- `X_t` (input, unit): anchor price level (~1000)
- `dX_t` (input, unit): anchor change that step
- `M_t` (state, unit): per-batch matched volume
- `M_t1` (state, unit): next matched volume
- `R_t` (state, unit): rebate pool level
- `R_t1` (state, unit): next rebate pool level
- `V_t` (state, unit): cumulative posted-depth tenure
- `V_t1` (state, unit): next depth tenure
- `u_t` (auxiliary, unit): out-of-batch relative anchor stress

- `u_t = abs(dX_t) / X_t` — out-of-batch relative anchor stress (sequencing cannot touch it)
- `M_t1 = clip(400.0 * theta * (1.0 - k_imp * 100.0 * u_t), 50.0, 400.0)` — matched volume shrinks with out-of-batch anchor stress, bounded
- `V_t1 = 0.9 * V_t + 0.1 * M_t1` — depth tenure accumulates posted depth — withdrawing providers decay theirs
- `R_t1 = clip(R_t + r0 + 0.02 * M_t1 + 0.01 * V_t1 - R_t * 0.05, 20.0, 3000.0)` — rebate pool pays from matched volume AND vested depth tenure — fair-weather re-posting earns less than continuous posting

**Parameters:** theta ∈ [0.05, 0.95] (default 0.4), k_imp ∈ [0.001, 0.1] (default 0.01), r0 ∈ [1.0, 100.0] (default 15.0)

**Open questions (§13):** should vesting weight recent depth more than the 0.9 EWMA does?

**Critical assumptions:** posted depth is refreshed each batch; impact scales linearly with relative anchor movement; rebates are paid from captured spread only
