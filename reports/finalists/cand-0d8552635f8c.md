## Problem

Oracle quorums weight all reporters equally or by governance fiat; mis-calibrated or captured feeds keep full influence until a vote removes them, and deviation carries no explicit price.

## Mechanism

The oracle's own quorum becomes a prediction market on itself: reporters stake on their deviation risk, and the realized median resolves the stake. Deviating feeds are penalized in money and down-weighted in influence by a deterministic rule, so manipulation must buy both the report AND the deviation stake — and the deviation stake loses when manipulation fails.

## Mathematical Model

- `X_t` (input, unit): anchor price level (~1000)
- `dX_t` (input, unit): anchor change that step
- `W_q` (state, unit): aggregate calibrated weight
- `W_q1` (state, unit): next aggregate weight
- `M_q` (state, unit): published quorum median
- `M_q1` (state, unit): next median

- `M_q1 = clip(M_q*(1-0.3) + 0.3*X_t, 300.0, 1700.0)` — published median EWMA-tracks the anchor (quorum smoothing)
- `W_q1 = clip(W_q*(1-0.12) + 0.12*(1000.0 + eta*sqrt(abs(M_q1-X_t)/max(X_t,1.0))*(X_t-M_q1)/max(abs(X_t-M_q1), 1.0)*(dX_t/max(abs(dX_t), 0.01))), 400.0, 2000.0)` — weight REWARDS deviation TOWARD the anchor's move (directional accuracy), punishing away-drift

**Parameters:** lambda_ ∈ [0.05, 0.8] (default 0.3), eta ∈ [10.0, 900.0] (default 200.0), fee ∈ [1.0, 60.0] (default 12.0)

**Open questions (§13):** can sandbagged disagreement stakes fake calibration?

**Critical assumptions:** reporter deviation is measurable against the realized median; calibration weighting is deterministic, no governance vote
