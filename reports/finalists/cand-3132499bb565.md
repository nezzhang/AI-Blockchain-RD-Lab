## Problem

Treasury portfolios drift from target allocations; single-sided auctions rebalance slowly and at stale prices

## Mechanism

Two simultaneous auctions (sell the overweight side, buy the underweight side) clear at a crossed spread; the rebalance size per round is bounded by a seasoning parameter so the treasury never needs to clear its whole deviation in one auction
