## Problem

On-chain insurance pools pay correlated claims from a shared float, draining solvent members' capital

## Mechanism

Claims clear in batches against an escrowed, partitioned pool: a deterministic partition rule allocates each member's share of the batch claim bill by exposure weight, and a solvency check defers the batch when the shared float falls below a floor, so correlated claims cannot overdraw the pool silently

## Mathematical Model

- `X_t` (input, unit): anchor price level (~1000)
- `dX_t` (input, unit): anchor change that step
- `L_t` (state, unit): shared escrow float level
- `L_t1` (state, unit): next float level
- `B_t` (state, unit): per-batch claim bill
- `B_t1` (state, unit): next claim bill
- `W_t` (state, unit): cumulative premium history
- `W_t1` (state, unit): next premium history
- `u_t` (auxiliary, unit): realized relative anchor stress
- `payable` (auxiliary, unit): underwriting-capped bill

- `u_t = abs(dX_t) / X_t` — realized relative anchor stress this step
- `B_t1 = clip(50.0 + chi * 300.0 * u_t, 20.0, 500.0)` — gross claim bill rises with relative anchor stress, bounded
- `payable = min(B_t1, omega * W_t)` — payable bill is capped at omega x the claimant pool's premium history — claim farming draws only on premiums already paid in
- `L_t1 = clip(L_t + prem - payable, 150.0, 5000.0)` — float accumulates premiums and pays the underwriting-capped bill
- `W_t1 = W_t + prem` — premium history grows with every premium step

**Parameters:** chi ∈ [0.01, 0.9] (default 0.3), prem ∈ [1.0, 200.0] (default 25.0), omega ∈ [0.1, 0.95] (default 0.8)

**Open questions (§13):** should W_t be per-claimant rather than pooled for stronger caps?

**Critical assumptions:** the batch claim bill is known before clearing; the float floor forces explicit recapitalization
