## Problem

Inference markets price per token or per request; the dominant operating cost (energy) is neither escrowed nor priced natively.

## Mechanism

Inference pricing becomes energy-native: escrow, fees, and bonds are all denominated in joules with an endogenous energy price index from realized mesh costs. Mispricing energy is prepaid through bonds, and the index self-corrects toward the true marginal cost of compute.

## Mathematical Model

- `X_t` (input, unit): anchor price level (~1000)
- `dX_t` (input, unit): anchor change that step
- `J_t` (state, unit): outstanding joule escrow
- `J_t1` (state, unit): next escrow
- `P_e` (state, unit): energy price index
- `P_e1` (state, unit): next energy price
- `a_t` (auxiliary, unit): over-attestation intensity

- `a_t = sqrt(max(0.0, abs(dX_t)/max(X_t,1.0) - 0.06) + 0.2*max(0.0, (X_t-P_e)/max(X_t,1.0) - 0.05))` — gap includes corroboration drift (delivered level vs energy price index): systematic top-of-band attestation drags the index and self-corrects
- `P_e1 = clip(P_e*(1-nu) + nu*(1000.0 + 400.0*abs(dX_t)/max(X_t,1.0)), 550.0, 2000.0)` — energy index EWMA of mesh realized cost intensity
- `J_t1 = clip(J_t + mu_j - 0.05*(J_t-1000.0) - psi*a_t*250.0, 500.0, 2200.0)` — escrow releases against attested joules; gaps slash bonds

**Parameters:** nu ∈ [0.05, 0.8] (default 0.25), psi ∈ [0.05, 0.9] (default 0.4), mu_j ∈ [3.0, 70.0] (default 18.0)

**Open questions (§13):** should regional energy heterogeneity split the index?

**Critical assumptions:** mesh energy cost intensity is proxied by |dX|/X; over-attestation beyond tolerance slashes bonds
